Information to Prepare for a Flight Inquiry

Having your basic trip information ready can make a flight inquiry easier and more efficient.

Useful information to prepare

  • Departure airport or city
  • Destination airport or city
  • Departure date
  • Return date, if applicable
  • Number of passengers
  • Preferred cabin class
  • Important travel preferences

United Airlines Flight Booking Help

If you are searching for help with a United Airlines flight inquiry, it is useful to know your approximate travel dates and whether your trip is one-way or round-trip.

Travelers should review applicable fare conditions, baggage rules, cancellation conditions and other restrictions before completing a purchase.

Before completing a booking

  • Confirm your travel dates.
  • Check the departure and arrival airports.
  • Confirm the number of travelers.
  • Review baggage requirements.
  • Review change and cancellation conditions.
  • Confirm the final price before payment.
  • Keep your booking documentation after purchase.
